WHO WE ARE

Making Waste Management Smarter

Waste management is an important part of maintaining clean and healthy communities. However, reporting garbage, requesting collection, and tracking waste disposal can be difficult. EcoClean aims to simplify this process through technology. Our platform provides a convenient way for people to report garbage problems and request waste collection while helping collectors manage their work efficiently.
